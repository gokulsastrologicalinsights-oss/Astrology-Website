# Medical Astrology: "ஜாதகத்தில் பொருந்துகிறது" (Chart-Matched Rules & Remedies)

## Overview & Mandate
Under the **மருத்துவ ஜோதிடம் (Medical Astrology)** module, the primary view is:
**"ஜாதகத்தில் பொருந்துகிறது" (Chart-Matched Rules & Prescribed Remedies)**.

### Architectural Principle
Whenever any rules, conditions, classical verses, or treatise books (Parashara, Phaladeepika, Saravali, KP System, Dr. S. Krishna Kumar, Dr. S. S. Chatterjee, Max Heindel, or Jane Ridder-Patrick) are updated or introduced into the system:
1. **Dynamic Inclusion**: The matching engine in `ChartMatchedRulesSection.tsx` automatically aggregates, filters, and displays all rules and conditions that match the native's specific horoscope.
2. **Actionable Remedies**: For every matched condition or afflicted planet, corresponding classical remedies (Mantra/Japa, Ayurvedic herbs and diet, metal-charged water therapy, charity/dana, cell salts, Bach flower remedies, and sacred temple propitiation) must be surfaced without hesitation.
3. **No Omissions**: Both protective vitality shields (Arishta Bhanga, Arogya, Parijata Yoga) and medical vulnerability combinations are analyzed and clearly categorized.

### 5. Jane Ridder-Patrick Compendium
- **Primary Source**: *A Handbook of Medical Astrology* (Second Edition, Revised and Enlarged, CrabApple Press, Edinburgh).
- **Author**: Jane Ridder-Patrick, Ph.D., N.D., M.N.I.M.H. (Naturopathic Physician and Medical Herbalist).
- **Features**:
  1. **The Astrological Alphabet Framework**:
     - *Planets*: **WHAT** is happening (physiological function, drive, or organ archetype).
     - *Signs*: **WHERE** in the physical body the action takes place.
     - *Aspects*: **HOW** the energies interact (harmonious integration vs friction, blockage, or overactivity).
     - *Houses*: **WHICH** area of life or circumstance is impacted (1st = body/constitution, 6th = acute illness/metabolic habits, 8th = crises/surgery, 12th = hospitalization/chronic vulnerability).
     - *Transits*: **WHEN** the potential manifests in time, triggering crises or therapeutic windows.
  2. **Decumbiture Strictures & Diagnostics (William Lilly & Nicholas Culpeper)**:
     - Ascendant radicality (< 3° embryonic/premature, > 27° chronic/past turning point, 3°–27° radical).
     - Moon in Via Combusta (15° Libra to 15° Scorpio) metabolic turbulence check.
     - Moon Void of Course and 7th house affliction (Saturn/Mars in 7th).
     - Ascendant lord combustion / Saturn in 1st house.
     - Hippocratic Critical Days cycle (Semi-square at ~3.5 days, Square at 7th day, Sesquiquadrate at 10.5 days, Opposition at 14th day).
     - Anatomical Sidedness (Eastern/Right vs Western/Left).
  3. **Exact Hermetic Arabian Parts Calculated for Native**:
     - **Part of Sickness** (Congenital/Chronic Weakness): Day: $\text{Asc} + \text{Mars} - \text{Saturn}$; Night: $\text{Asc} + \text{Saturn} - \text{Mars}$.
     - **Part of Surgery** (Surgical Trauma Risk): $\text{Asc} + \text{Saturn} - \text{Mars}$.
     - **Part of Death & Critical Endurance**: $\text{Asc} + 240^\circ - \text{Moon}$.
  4. **The Three Crosses Reflex Dynamics**:
     - *Cardinal Cross* (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn): Regulation & Acute Response (Brain, Digestion, Renal, Skeletal/Skin).
     - *Fixed Cross* (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius): Vital Stability & Resistance (Thyroid, Cardiac, Reproductive/Excretory, Circulation).
     - *Mutable Cross* (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces): Metabolic Exchange & Nervous Transmission (Respiration/Nerves, Assimilation, Hepatic/Locomotion, Lymphatics/Immune).
  5. **Elemental Balance & Materia Medica Therapeutics**:
     - Constitutional temperament profiling (Fire/Pitta/Bilious, Earth/Melancholic, Air/Vata/Sanguine, Water/Kapha/Phlegmatic).
     - Dietary advice and indicated botanical classes to restore homeostasis.
  6. **12 Biochemic Cell Salts (Dr. W. H. Carey / Inez Eudora Perry)**:
     - Exact cell salt, abbreviation, and physiological action for all 12 signs, highlighting the native's active Ascendant and Moon signs.
  7. **12 Bach Flower Remedies (Peter Damian)**:
     - Soul-temperament flower essences corresponding to the 12 signs (Impatiens, Gentian, Cerato, Clematis, Vervain, Centaury, Scleranthus, Chicory, Agrimony, Mimulus, Water Violet, Rock Rose).
  8. **12 Acupuncture Meridians**:
     - Meridian correspondences across the 12 zodiac signs highlighting native's active Ascendant.
  9. **Nicholas Culpeper's 7 Rules for Astrological Therapeutics**:
     - Foundational principles of astro-herbalism, antipathy vs sympathy treatment, and physiological pacing.
  10. **Three Stages of Astrological Healing**:
     - Stage 1: Identification & Acknowledgment.
     - Stage 2: Exploration & Understanding.
     - Stage 3: Upgrading & Conscious Integration.
  11. **24 Classical & Clinical Rules Registry**:
     - Comprehensive rule set with active status evaluation, English and Tamil explanations, and constitutional remedies.
