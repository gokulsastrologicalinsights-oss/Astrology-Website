1. **Clockwise Progression**: All regular planets traverse clockwise from $0^\circ$ to $30^\circ$.

   - Direct planets at $27^\circ - 30^\circ$: Impart full results to the **subsequent (next) house**.

   - Retrograde planets at $27^\circ - 30^\circ$: Radiate impact into the **previous house**.
